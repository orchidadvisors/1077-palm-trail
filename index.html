A turnkey coastal cottage with a heated saltwater pool on a quiet Boynton Beach street, a short bike ride to the sand and minutes to Delray's Atlantic Avenue.

An original white-brick arch frames one bright, open living and dining space: crisp white walls, wide-plank floors and a white shaker kitchen with quartz counters and stainless appliances. Prefer it fully open? The arch can come out for one continuous great room.

Out back, a new covered open-air lanai with a plank ceiling, fan, wall-mounted television and built-in grill island flows onto the travertine deck and a heated saltwater pool with a tanning platform, framed by clusia hedges, foxtail palms and landscape lighting for evenings outdoors.

Two bedrooms, a spa-style bath with a glass walk-in shower, rooftop solar, a new white privacy fence, and an easy ride to Oceanfront Park, the Boynton Harbor Marina and downtown Delray.
